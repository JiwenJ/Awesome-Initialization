## Placement Rules

- Put reading guides, method explanations, and practical research guidance in docs/.
- Put BibTeX references and bibliography instructions in papers/.
- Put search strategies, source-check logs, update histories, excluded candidates, and unresolved leads in maintenance/.
- Keep research limitations with the relevant paper or guide; keep the process used to discover and verify sources here.
- Rebase relative links and check section anchors when moving files. Keep paper, resource, and artifact tables synchronized between the README and their guides.
